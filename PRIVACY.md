# Ciwaan Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 27, 2026**

Ciwaan is a digital-addressing and navigation service. This policy explains what information the app processes, why it processes it, and the choices available to users.

## 1. No account required
Ciwaan does not require an account, password, email address, phone number, or user profile to create or find a Ciwaan.

## 2. Location information
Ciwaan requests device location permission only when a feature needs it. Creating a Ciwaan can use the current GPS location to place the map. Guide Me uses the device's live location while navigation is active so the app can display your position, route, distance, progress, and arrival.

If you deny permission, features that require your current position will not work, but the app can still be used to look up and view shared Ciwaans.

## 3. Address information
When you create a Ciwaan, the service stores the Ciwaan code, place name, selected latitude and longitude, exact/general precision choice, and timestamps. A Ciwaan is intentionally shareable. Anyone with a valid code or link can retrieve the location associated with that address.

Do not create an exact Ciwaan for a private location unless you intend to share its precise location.

## 4. General location
General location is designed to reduce the precision of the location you publish. It does not guarantee anonymity. Users should choose the least precise setting that still serves their purpose.

## 5. Live navigation
Ciwaan does not need to create a permanent history of your live movement to provide Guide Me. The device location is used while navigation is active. Route calculations may transmit the route endpoints needed to calculate a route through the routing infrastructure used by the app.

## 6. Third-party infrastructure
The app uses map tiles and routing services operated by third parties. Those services can receive technical requests and geographic coordinates required to render maps or calculate routes. Their own privacy policies and usage terms apply to those requests.

## 7. Anti-abuse information
To reduce automated address creation, the backend may process a short-lived one-way hash derived from the network address of a request. It is used for rate limiting, not advertising or profiling, and is designed to expire after a short retention period.

## 8. Security
Ciwaan keeps public address records behind a server-side API and uses database row-level security. The credential used to manage a no-account-created Ciwaan is returned once to the creating device and stored locally; it is not published as part of the public address record.

## 9. Sharing and third parties
Ciwaan does not sell users' location data. A user can intentionally disclose a location by sharing a Ciwaan. Once a location is shared, Ciwaan cannot control what a recipient does with the information.

## 10. Children
Ciwaan is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 11. Changes
This policy may be updated when the service, technology, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.

## 12. Contact
Privacy requests should use the current contact method published with the Ciwaan service or its official distribution listing.
